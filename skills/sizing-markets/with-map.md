# A market sizing and a competitive map together

One run builds both reports on one company list, in one `conversation_id`. The map's research runs while the sizing's top-down and growth jobs run, so the sizing's attribution later finds much of its research already done. Each phase follows its own skill's part and rules; this part sets only the order and where the two meet.

## Scoping

Run sizing phase 1 and map phase 0 as one: one grid, one message of at most four questions, then `grasp_set_market_sizing_scope` and the map's header to `grasp_write_competitive_map_page`. Where the two meet:

- The value-chain row names the layer the sizing counts and the stages the map shows. A stage beyond that layer is an adjacent cell for the sizing and in scope for the map.
- One setting answers both the HQ rule and the foreign-players question.
- The map's purpose is the question that follows from the sizing's, stated in the message.
- The plan: both reports in about 45 to 90 minutes, this message the only pause for the user, each page's link shared as it is done and one summary after.

## Order

1. Sizing phase 2, through the coverage check.
2. Map phases 1 to 3, then phase 4 through sending the positions to the page.
3. Sizing phases 3 to 5.
4. The rest of map phases 4 and 5.

## Closing

Once the map page is done, one message closes both reports, in place of each output part's own closing: the sizing's headline as the reconciliation prints it, with its range; the map's answer in two or three findings; the choices made after scoping, at most four across both reports, each in one line with why. Give both pages' links, offer at most three next steps across both, and end with one invitation to challenge or deepen either page.
